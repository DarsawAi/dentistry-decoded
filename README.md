# Dentistry Decoded 🦷✨

> **Demystifying modern dental science.** Evidence-based insights, breakthrough technology, and practical oral health tips for clinicians, students, and patients.

---

## Brand Identity & Overview
- **Brand Name:** Dentistry Decoded
- **Handle:** `@DentistDecoded` / `@DentistryDecode`
- **Primary Platforms:** Facebook Page & Twitter/X
- **Color Palette:** Dental Lilac/Violet, Royal Purple, Clean Slate Navy, Luminous White
- **Target Audience:**
  1. Dental Patients (seeking clear, honest, non-intimidating explanations)
  2. Dental Students & Young Clinicians (seeking evidence-based literature and career insights)
  3. Practicing Dentists & Specialists (engaging in clinical technology discussions)
