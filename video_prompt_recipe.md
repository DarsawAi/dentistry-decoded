# Dentistry Decoded: NotebookLM Video Prompt Blueprint

This document records the exact, approved formula for generating high-converting, strictly fact-grounded NotebookLM prompts for both short clips/reels and full-length YouTube videos.

---

## 🎯 Core Principles
1. **100% Strict Factual Grounding:** Rely only on the specific paper's data, quantitative synthesis, and explicit findings. Zero outside extrapolations.
2. **Balanced Nuance:** Always pair technological advantages with scientific limitations (e.g., in vitro vs in vivo, prospective trial needs, technical gaps).
3. **The Human Factor:** Emphasize the irreplaceable expertise of clinicians, dental technicians, and healthcare workers. AI is an assistant/co-pilot, never a replacement.
4. **Realistic Visual Direction:** Specify authentic clinical environments, true dental CAD/CAM displays, and realistic prosthetics/radiographs—strictly avoid cartoonish or exaggerated sci-fi tropes.
5. **Standard Outro:** Conclude with the educational/medical disclaimer and a warm subscribe/like call-to-action.

---

## 📱 Format 1: Short Video / Reels / Shorts Prompt Template

```text
You are producing a concise, professional video overview summarizing the attached source document: "[PAPER TITLE]" ([AUTHORS, YEAR, JOURNAL]).

Follow these strict rules and guidelines:

1. STRICT FACTUAL GROUNDING (NO OUTSIDE CLAIMS):
- Rely 100% strictly on the data and findings reported in this specific paper.
- Do NOT introduce unmentioned clinical maxims, outside statistics, or speculative claims.
- Stick strictly to the paper's quantitative synthesis ([KEY SAMPLE/STUDY METRICS]).

2. KEY ADVANTAGES OF AI TO HIGHLIGHT:
- [Primary Advantage 1: Efficiency, Speed, or Specificity with p-value/numbers]
- [Primary Advantage 2: Diagnostic Accuracy, Fit, or Metric range]
- [Primary Advantage 3: Key clinical feature]

3. CRITICAL LIMITATIONS & SCIENTIFIC REALITY CHECK:
- Explicitly state the authors' primary caveat ([e.g. in vitro predominance, need for clinical trials, lack of long-term follow-up]).
- Note any specific clinical or technical gaps ([e.g. dynamic forces, dataset heterogeneity]).

4. THE CRITICAL ROLE OF CLINICIANS & HUMAN EXPERTISE:
- Strongly emphasize that AI is NOT replacing human dental professionals.
- Highlight the necessity of clinician oversight and human clinical judgment.

5. TONE, PACING & VISUAL DIRECTION:
- Tone: Professional, objective, engaging, and science-grounded.
- Visual Aesthetics: Realistic, high-resolution clinical dentistry imagery. Show true-to-life dental instruments, genuine scans, and realistic laboratory workflows. Avoid cartoonish, flashy, or exaggerated sci-fi graphics.
```

## 📺 Format 2: Full-Length YouTube Video Prompt Template

```text
You are producing a comprehensive, long-form educational YouTube video overview (standard landscape 16:9 format, full discussion—not a short or clip) analyzing the attached peer-reviewed paper: "[PAPER TITLE]" ([AUTHORS, YEAR, JOURNAL]).

Follow these comprehensive production guidelines:

1. STRICT FACTUAL ACCURACY & EVIDENCE BASE:
- Base every single clinical and technical claim strictly on the findings of this specific systematic review and meta-analysis. Do NOT extrapolate or introduce external, unverified claims.
- Detail the methodology and quantitative scope: [Number of studies, databases searched, screening criteria, and quantitative meta-analysis groups].

2. IN-DEPTH ANALYSIS OF AI ADVANTAGES & EFFICIENCY:
- [Detailed Advantage 1: Workflow acceleration, time savings, or sensitivity/specificity metrics with exact p-values].
- [Detailed Advantage 2: Statistical meta-analysis outcomes, standardized mean differences, or AUC metrics].
- [Detailed Advantage 3: Clinical thresholds, precision parameters, and comparative benchmarks against conventional workflows].

3. CRITICAL SCIENTIFIC LIMITATIONS & REALITY CHECK:
- Provide an honest, thorough reality check as emphasized by the study's authors: [nature of evidence, e.g. in vitro / computational nature, lack of prospective randomized trials].
- Technical gaps: [e.g. dynamic occlusion, lighting variations, edge cases, single-center limitations].

4. THE INDISPENSABLE ROLE OF DENTAL PROFESSIONALS & HUMAN EXPERTISE:
- Strongly emphasize that AI is an adjunctive accelerator, NOT a replacement for human clinicians, specialists, or dental technicians.
- Explore collaborative human-AI workflows where clinician oversight, tactile judgment, complex anatomical management, and patient communication remain indispensable.

5. VISUAL DIRECTION FOR VIDEO SEGMENTS:
- Visuals must be realistic and scientifically grounded. Depict real clinical dental operatories, authentic software displays, high-precision equipment, and genuine biological/prosthetic elements. Avoid flashy neon, cartoonish graphics, or exaggerated sci-fi tropes.

6. OUTRO, DISCLAIMER & CALL TO ACTION:
- Outro Closing: Conclude with a clear summary that technology enhances speed and precision, but human clinical expertise remains the foundation of dentistry.
- Mandatory Educational Disclaimer: State clearly to listeners that this presentation is strictly for educational and informational purposes, and does not replace professional dental examination, diagnosis, or personalized clinical treatment planning.
- Call to Action: Warmly invite viewers to like the video, leave a comment with their thoughts on the topic, and subscribe to the Dentistry Decoded channel for weekly, peer-reviewed dental science breakdowns.
```

## 📝 YouTube Metadata Deliverables Checklist
Whenever generating for YouTube, always provide:
1. Three high-CTR, clickable title options.
2. Complete video description with:
   - Hook paragraph
   - "What You'll Learn" bullet points
   - Timestamps (0:00 to outro)
   - Primary research citation & DOI link
   - Website link to article
   - Medical/Educational disclaimer box
   - Subscribe call-to-action
   - Relevant hashtags
